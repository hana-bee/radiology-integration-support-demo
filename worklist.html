How the RIS Worklist fits into the workflow

HIS creates the patient/order information. The order can be transferred to RIS using HL7. RIS manages the radiology workflow and makes the examination information available to the modality. The imaging system can then use this information to perform the correct examination and send images to PACS using DICOM.
